in desert dust stuck to sweaty skin after collapsing at the summit 
and the beautiful bleached hard work bruises earned in between 

teetering thin edges of oil smears from broken car door frames 
or soaked into callouses from late night headlamp lit fixes

carved in mountain side rock slides
that crinkle like poetry lines 

and again while uncovering the soft crumbles of earth that curve the creek i knew as a kid 
ones full of the same unchartedness only a childhood holds

in the marigold garden beds on my morning walk
the kind you want to grow into 

but most certainly 
amid the pools of haphazard spills thundered from turbulence 
hoarding around a hot coffee and cream
on the flight home
